The real problem

Your website may be generating leads. Your process may still be losing them.

If you run a service business, firm or agency, more traffic rarely fixes this. The leak is usually follow-up, CRM use, routing or ownership.

  • Form submissions go to email, then disappear
  • CRM usage is inconsistent
  • Good leads are not clearly qualified
  • Response times vary too much
  • Follow-up depends on memory
  • Leadership cannot see what is converting

If two or three of these sound familiar, the leak is in the process, not the website. That is exactly what we fix.

Where AI actually helps

AI is optional and small here. We use it for a handful of repetitive jobs inside your pipeline. You don’t need any AI tools to start, and the process matters more than the tech.

AI supports people and process. It does not replace judgment.

Questions

Straight answers

Do we need a new CRM?

No. We work in the CRM you already use. If it truly can’t do the job, we’ll say so in the audit. If you don’t have one, we’ll help you choose a simple one.

Is this a website redesign?

No. We look at the parts of your site that affect lead capture, such as forms, booking paths and key pages, and fix those. A full redesign is a separate project, and we’ll tell you honestly if you need one.

Is this custom software development?

No. It is consulting and implementation: process design, configuration and light automation in tools you already have or can easily adopt. We don’t build custom SaaS.

Do we need AI tools already?

No. The system works without AI. Where AI helps, it handles small jobs such as drafting a first reply or flagging stalled deals, and a person stays in charge.

How quickly can this be launched?

The rollout is 30 days from kickoff. It stays on schedule when we get access to your tools quickly and one person on your side can make decisions.

Who is this best for?

Service-based businesses, professional services firms, consultants and agencies in Boulder and Denver that get leads from web, referral or outreach but follow up inconsistently. Elsewhere in Colorado or remote? Ask, and we’ll tell you if it’s a fit.
